# Huong Dan Su Dung A La Carte Cho Nhan Vien Moi

Ngay cap nhat: 2026-06-22

## 1. Muc dich

Tai lieu nay dung de huong dan nhan vien moi thao tac nhanh voi module `Nha hang A La Carte`.

Muc tieu:

- mo phong cho khach vang lai
- cau hinh so ban phuc vu
- them mon theo nhom ban hoac ban le
- gui bep / bar
- sua mon neu nhap nham
- thanh toan dung quy trinh

## 2. Cac khai niem can nho

### 2.1. Phong

La phong an hoac khoang an ma khach se su dung.

### 2.2. Ban Tong

La noi quan ly thong tin chung cua ca doan:

- ten khach
- so dien thoai
- tong so khach
- gio den
- tong tien cua ca phong

Ban Tong khong phai noi chinh de tang giam tung dong mon nhu ban le.

### 2.3. Ban thuong

La cac ban vat ly trong phong:

- ban dang phuc vu
- ban cho
- ban trong

### 2.4. Nhom ban

La nhom cac ban co cach len mon giong nhau.

Vi du:

- Ban 01 + Ban 02 cung menu, moi ban 10 khach
- Ban 03 la ban le 11 khach

## 3. Luong thao tac nhanh cho khach vang lai

### 3.1. Mo phong

1. Vao tab `Khu vuc`.
2. Chon tang / khu vuc neu can.
3. Nhay dup vao phong trong phu hop suc chua.
4. He thong se tao don moi va chuyen sang man hinh van hanh.

### 3.2. Cau hinh thong tin khach

1. Tai khu `Ban cua phong`, nhay dup vao `Ban Tong`.
2. Nhap:
   - ten khach
   - so dien thoai
   - tong so khach
   - so ban mo
   - ghi chu neu co
3. Bam `Luu mo ban`.

### 3.3. Kiem tra ket qua chia ban

Sau khi luu:

- cac ban dang phuc vu se hien mau trang thai phuc vu
- cac ban chua can dung se o trang thai ban cho
- Ban Tong se hien tong so khach va tong so ban

Neu khach tang them:

1. Chon ban dang o trang thai cho.
2. Nhay dup hoac mo cau hinh ban.
3. Mo them ban phuc vu theo thuc te.

## 4. Cach them mon

### 4.1. Them mon theo nhom ban

Dung trong truong hop:

- nhieu ban an giong nhau
- can len mon nhanh cho ca doan

Thao tac:

1. Chon `Ban Tong` hoac chon mot ban bat ky trong phong.
2. Bam `Them mon / nhom ban`.
3. Trong popup:
   - cot trai: chon mon
   - cot giua: chon nhom ban / pham vi ap dung
   - cot phai: xem preview truoc khi gui
4. Bam `Soan nhom ban` tai mon can them.
5. Chon pham vi ap dung:
   - tat ca ban phuc vu
   - nhom ban chuan
   - nhom ban le
   - ban dang chon
6. Bam `Luu & chuyen buoc gui` neu can chot preview.
7. Bam `Gui bep/bar` de gui phieu.

### 4.2. Them mon cho ban le

Dung trong truong hop:

- chi mot ban co mon rieng
- ban le co dinh luong khac

Thao tac:

1. Chon dung ban le o khu card ben trai.
2. Ben panel chi tiet ben phai bam `Them mon / nhom ban`.
3. Chon mon va ap dung cho `Ban dang chon`.
4. Kiem tra preview.
5. Gui bep/bar.

## 5. Cach doc panel chi tiet ben phai

Panel ben phai hien thong tin theo ban dang chon.

### 5.1. Neu dang chon Ban Tong

Danh sach mon se hien tong SL cua ca phong.

Vi du:

- Bac xiu x52

Dieu nay co nghia la tong tat ca cac ban trong phong dang dung 52 ly, khong tach tung ban ngay o danh sach tong.

### 5.2. Neu dang chon ban le

Danh sach mon chi hien mon cua dung ban do.

Vi du:

- Ban 01: Bac xiu x11
- Ban 03: Bac xiu x10

## 6. Sua hoac xoa mon

### 6.1. Sua mon o ban le

Tai cot `Tac vu`:

- `Sua`: mo popup sua tong so luong / ghi chu / ingredient-level neu co
- `Xoa`: xoa dong mon neu nhap nham

Neu mon chua gui bep:

- co the sua binh thuong

Neu mon da gui bep:

- he thong tao phieu dieu chinh tang / giam
- can nhap ly do dieu chinh

### 6.2. Sua mon o Ban Tong

Tai `Ban Tong`, nut `Sua` dung de sua tong the cho ca phong, khong dung `+/-` nhu ban le.

Dung khi:

- can chinh lai tong SL cua mon cho ca doan
- can gop lai so luong neu truoc do nhap lap

Vi du:

- Ca phe sua dang bi nhap thanh x156
- Thuc te chi can x52
- Vao `Ban Tong` -> bam `Sua` dong `Ca phe sua` -> nhap lai tong SL dung -> luu

## 7. Gui bep / bar

### 7.1. Khi nao can gui

Can gui khi:

- da chot dot mon
- da kiem tra preview
- da chon dung nhom ban / ban le

### 7.2. Sau khi gui

He thong se:

- tao phieu bep / bar
- cap nhat trang thai dong mon
- tru kho theo recipe neu mon co cong thuc

### 7.3. Xem lich su gui

Vao tab `Thong tin` cua panel chi tiet de xem:

- ma phieu
- tram xu ly
- loai phieu send / cancel
- gio gui
- cac dong mon da gui

## 8. Thanh toan

### 8.1. Chon phuong thuc

Tai panel ben phai chon mot trong cac nut:

- `Tien mat`
- `Chuyen khoan`
- `Cong no`

### 8.2. Hoan tat

1. Kiem tra lai tong tien.
2. Bam `Thanh toan`.
3. Neu can huy don thi bam `Huy don`.

## 9. Cai dat may in bep / bar

Vao `Cai dat` -> `May in / Bep bar`.

### 9.1. Cach nhap may in

Co 2 cach:

- nhap dung ten may in Windows, vi du `XP-80C`
- nhap IP may in mang, vi du `192.168.1.50` hoac `192.168.1.50:9100`

### 9.2. Kiem tra

Bam:

- `In thu bep`
- `In thu bar`
- `In thu hoa don`

Neu in thanh cong, trang thai se bao da gui lenh in thu.

## 10. Quan tri nhom phuc vu

Vao `Cai dat` -> `Nhom phuc vu`.

Dung khi can:

- tao nhom ban chuan
- tao nhom ban le
- dat rule mon cho nhom
- gom nhung ban co cach phuc vu giong nhau

Luu y:

- nhom he thong khong xoa duoc
- nhom tuy chinh thi co the tao, sua, xoa

## 11. Xu ly cac tinh huong thuong gap

### 11.1. Khach tang them nguoi

Thao tac:

1. Chon `Ban Tong`.
2. Mo cau hinh ban.
3. Tang tong khach hoac mo them ban cho thanh ban phuc vu.
4. Neu mon da len theo nhom ban thi them dot mon bo sung va gui lai bep/bar.

### 11.2. Khach doi mon sau khi da gui bep

Thao tac:

1. Chon dung ban.
2. Bam `Sua` tren dong mon.
3. Nhap ly do dieu chinh.
4. Luu lai de he thong sinh phieu dieu chinh.

### 11.3. Nhan vien nhap trung mon

Neu nhap trung cho ca phong:

1. Chon `Ban Tong`.
2. Bam `Sua` tai dong mon bi trung.
3. Chinh lai tong SL dung.

Neu nhap trung o mot ban:

1. Chon ban do.
2. Bam `Sua` hoac `Xoa` dong mon.

## 12. Nguyen tac thao tac de tranh sai sot

- Luon kiem tra dang chon `Ban Tong` hay `Ban le` truoc khi them mon.
- Goi mon chung cho ca doan thi thao tac theo nhom ban.
- Mon dac biet, ban le, phan khac dinh luong thi thao tac tren ban cu the.
- Mon da gui bep khong sua tay bang cach nhap de, phai qua popup sua de co ly do.
- Truoc khi thanh toan, doi chieu lai danh sach mon va tong tien o panel ben phai.

## 13. Checklist cho nhan vien moi trong 1 ca thao tac

1. Kiem tra may in bep / bar da in thu duoc chua.
2. Mo phong dung suc chua.
3. Nhap dung tong so khach.
4. Kiem tra so ban phuc vu he thong chia ra.
5. Them mon theo nhom ban truoc.
6. Them mon rieng cho ban le sau.
7. Kiem tra preview roi moi gui bep/bar.
8. Khi sua mon da gui, phai nhap ly do.
9. Truoc khi thanh toan, doi chieu panel chi tiet va tong tien.

## 14. Ghi chu cho quan ly / thu ngan

- Neu nhan vien bao khong in duoc, vao `Cai dat` kiem tra lai ten may in hoac IP may in.
- Neu thay tong mon o Ban Tong khong hop ly, uu tien kiem tra xem co nhap trung dot mon hay khong.
- Neu phong da mo nhung chua nhap thong tin khach, don co the dang o trang thai `Draft`.
